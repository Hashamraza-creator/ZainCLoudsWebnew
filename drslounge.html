Portfolio Details

Drs lounge

DRS Lounge is a comprehensive restaurant management system designed to streamline both user and admin experiences. For users, the app offers a seamless browsing and ordering interface, enabling customers to easily explore the menu, place orders, customize meals, and make secure payments. For restaurant admins, it provides powerful tools to manage orders, update menus, track sales, and monitor customer feedback, all within an intuitive dashboard. Whether you're a customer enjoying your meal or an admin ensuring smooth operations, DRS Lounge brings efficiency, convenience, and enhanced service to the dining experience
